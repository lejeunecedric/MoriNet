<threat_model>
  <threat id="T-18-03" stride="Tampering" severity="low">
    A malformed or hostile okf_json string persisted by a buggy writer could crash every later reader (badge, API). Mitigation: the store treats the column as opaque TEXT (no SQL-side parsing); every READER (18-03 badge, 18-05 API) must serde-parse defensively and fall back to "no badge / no okf field" on Err — pinned as a must-have in those plans. Tests: `vault_okf_roundtrip` proves byte-exact storage; unknown-slug and never-scanned collapse to one None shape.
  </threat>
  <threat id="T-18-04" stride="Information Disclosure" severity="low">
    `vault_okf` returning a distinguishable error for "unknown slug" vs "never scanned" would leak vault existence to unauthorized callers. Mitigation: both collapse to `Ok(None)` (same no-existence-leak rule as api::source_dir, Phase 16 D-02). Tests: `vault_okf_none_for_unknown_slug`, `vault_okf_none_when_never_scanned` assert identical shape.
  </threat>
</threat_model>
