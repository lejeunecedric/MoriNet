   from `doe` (day-of-era) instead of `doy` (day-of-year) — Hinnant
   `civil_from_days` transcription error. `fmt_date(1762400000)` renders
   `"2025-11-9137"`. Affects token-created dates (`:1345`) and `:1339`.

   dates. `epoch_secs_to_utc_date` (:551) is correct — do not touch.
